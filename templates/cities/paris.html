Paris

History

The Romans originally named it Lutetia, which was renamed Paris in the 4th century. It became the capital of France in the 10th century. Major events include the French Revolution (1789), occupation during World War II, and the 1968 student protests.

The metro opened in 1900. Today it ranks among the world's most extensive subway systems, with stations often decorated with themes reflecting local history.

The Eiffel Tower was originally intended as a temporary structure. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, it was designed to last only 20 years. Gustave Eiffel persuaded authorities to preserve the tower by demonstrating its practical value in scientific experiments, including radio transmission.
